Children's Liver Health

Early detection of liver problems in children is crucial for successful treatment.

Newborn Babies

0-28 days

Warning Signs

  • Yellowing of skin/eyes (jaundice)
  • Sweet-smelling breath
  • Swollen abdomen
  • Poor feeding
  • Dark urine or pale stools

Treatment Options

  • Phototherapy for jaundice
  • Liver function medications
  • Kasai procedure if needed
  • IV fluids and nutrition

Infants

1-12 months

Warning Signs

  • Poor weight gain
  • Failure to thrive
  • Persistent jaundice
  • Excessive sleepiness
  • Vomiting and irritability

Treatment Options

  • Specialized formula feeding
  • Vitamin supplements
  • Regular monitoring
  • Dietary modifications

Toddlers & Children

1-12 years

Warning Signs

  • Delayed growth
  • Chronic fatigue
  • Abdominal pain
  • Easy bruising
  • Behavioral changes

Treatment Options

  • Liver-friendly diet
  • Antiviral medications
  • Physical therapy
  • Liver transplant (severe)

When to Seek Immediate Medical Help

Sudden severe abdominal pain
Vomiting blood
Confusion or extreme drowsiness
Rapidly spreading jaundice
