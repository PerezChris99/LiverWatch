Liver-Friendly Recipes

Delicious Ugandan recipes that support liver health.

Steamed Tilapia with Vegetables

30 mins Low Fat

Ingredients

  • • 1 whole tilapia (cleaned)
  • • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • • 1 onion, sliced
  • • Fresh dodo (amaranth)
  • • Lemon juice, ginger, garlic

Instructions

  1. Season fish with lemon, ginger, garlic
  2. Layer vegetables in steaming pot
  3. Place fish on vegetables
  4. Steam for 20-25 minutes

Liver Benefits: High protein, low fat, rich in omega-3 fatty acids

Healthy Bean Katogo

45 mins High Fiber

Ingredients

  • • 2 cups dried beans (soaked)
  • • 3 green bananas (matooke)
  • • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • • 1 onion, chopped
  • • 1 tbsp olive oil, turmeric

Instructions

  1. Cook beans until almost tender
  2. Sauté onions and tomatoes in olive oil
  3. Add peeled matooke and beans
  4. Simmer with turmeric until done

Liver Benefits: Plant protein, fiber aids digestion, turmeric reduces inflammation

Obushera (Millet Porridge)

20 mins Energy Boost

Ingredients

  • • 1 cup millet flour
  • • 4 cups water
  • • Fresh lemon juice
  • • Small amount of honey (optional)
  • • Pinch of ginger

Instructions

  1. Mix millet flour with cold water
  2. Boil remaining water
  3. Slowly add mixture, stirring constantly
  4. Add lemon and ginger, simmer 10 mins

Liver Benefits: Whole grain, gluten-free, rich in B vitamins for liver function

Liver Detox Green Smoothie

5 mins Detox

Ingredients

  • • Handful of spinach/nakati
  • • 1 ripe papaya, cubed
  • • 1/2 lemon juice
  • • 1 cup water or coconut water
  • • Small piece of fresh ginger

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to blender
  2. Blend until smooth
  3. Add ice if desired
  4. Drink immediately for best benefits

Liver Benefits: Antioxidants, vitamin C, enzymes that support liver detoxification
