Specialized support for biliary atresia, cirrhosis, and liver failure - conditions that require urgent attention and expert care.

Time Critical

Biliary Atresia

Infant Liver Emergency

Rare but serious condition in newborns where bile ducts don't form properly. Surgery must happen before 60-90 days old.

Warning Signs:
  • Jaundice beyond 2 weeks
  • Dark/brown urine in baby
  • Pale/white stools
