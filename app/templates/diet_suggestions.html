Nutrition

Liver-Friendly Diet Guide

Proper nutrition is essential for liver health and recovery.

Foods to Eat

Leafy Greens

Spinach, kale, dodo (amaranth), nakati - rich in antioxidants

Lean Proteins

Tilapia, Nile perch, chicken breast, beans, groundnuts

Whole Grains

Millet, sorghum, brown rice, whole wheat posho

Fresh Fruits

Papaya, mangoes, watermelon, oranges, passion fruit

Foods to Avoid

Alcohol

All alcoholic beverages including local brews like waragi and tonto

Fried Foods

Deep fried foods, excessive cooking oil, fatty meats

Excess Sugar

Sodas, sweetened drinks, sugary snacks, too much honey

High Sodium

Processed foods, excessive salt, packaged snacks

Daily Tips

1

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of clean water daily

2

Eat Small, Frequent Meals

5-6 small meals instead of 3 large ones reduces liver strain

3

Coffee in Moderation

1-2 cups of coffee daily may help protect the liver
