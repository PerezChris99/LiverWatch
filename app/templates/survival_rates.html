Liver Disease in Uganda

High Hepatitis B Prevalence

Uganda has one of the highest rates of Hepatitis B in the world, affecting approximately 4.3% of the population.

Limited Access to Care

Many rural areas lack specialized liver care, making early detection and treatment challenging.

Vaccination Progress

Uganda's national immunization program now includes Hepatitis B vaccine for all infants, reducing future cases.

Key Takeaways

Early detection dramatically improves survival rates for all liver conditions

Hepatitis B and C are now highly treatable with modern medications

Lifestyle changes can reverse early-stage fatty liver disease

Regular screening is essential for those with risk factors
