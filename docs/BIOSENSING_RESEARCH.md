# Non-Invasive Biosensing Research Boundary

LiverWatch supports a research interface for sweat, interstitial-fluid and skin-interfaced sensing. This is deliberately separate from the validated clinical measurement domain.

Skin tags are not treated as sensors and cannot directly measure ALT, AST, bilirubin, ammonia or other liver function markers. A future skin-worn system may instead sample sweat/interstitial fluid or physiological signals and investigate correlations with reference measurements.

Evidence progression is: research → calibration → reference-lab comparison → clinical correlation → prospective validation → validated use. Until the final stage, outputs remain experimental and cannot trigger a diagnostic claim.
